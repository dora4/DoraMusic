Privacy Policy

Thank you for using Dora Music. We attach great importance to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information, as well as the purposes of the permissions required by the app.

1. Permission Descriptions

  • Network permissions (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, etc.): Used to connect to servers, download and stream music content, and check network status.
  • Storage permissions (READ/WRITE/MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used to read and save music files and cache on your device to improve the playback experience.
  • Audio permissions (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS, BLUETOOTH, etc.): Used to control audio playback, connect Bluetooth headsets, and support features such as auto-pause when Bluetooth disconnects.
  • Notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to send playback status, update reminders, and other push notifications.
  • NFC permission (NFC): Used to recognize and read/write NFC music disc tags, enabling features such as associating songs with NFC records, tap-to-play, and writing card-bound tracks. It will not read or upload other unrelated personal data from other NFC cards.
  • Other permissions (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, etc.): Used to keep the app running during music playback and prevent device sleep from interrupting playback.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect your sensitive personal information; all permissions are used solely to fulfill app functionality. The information we collect includes but is not limited to device information and app usage logs, which are used to optimize product experience and services.

3. Data Security

We employ industry-standard technologies to protect the security of your data and prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or tampering.

4. Your Rights

You can manage app permissions in your device settings. Disabling unnecessary permissions may affect the use of certain features.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be promptly published within the app or on the official website, and we encourage you to review it periodically.
